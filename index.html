Bookmark Revive AI · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Overview

Bookmark Revive AI ("the extension") is a Chrome new tab extension that helps you rediscover forgotten bookmarks. It is local-first: the developer runs no servers and does not collect, receive, or sell any of your data.

What data the extension reads and changes

  • Bookmarks (bookmarks permission): titles, URLs and folders are read to display, search and rotate them on the new tab page. Bookmarks are changed only when you act: adding a Favorite with "Also save to Chrome bookmarks" creates a bookmark in the folder you choose; editing a Favorite with sync enabled updates that bookmark's URL; clicking "Delete" on a card and confirming twice deletes that bookmark.
  • Most visited sites (topSites permission): the top 8 are used as initial Favorites on first install, and to adjust weighting if you turn on "boost frequently visited sites".
  • Site icons (favicon permission): loaded through Chrome's built-in API; your URLs are never sent to any third party for icons.
  • Alarms (alarms permission): used only while "Generate summaries for all bookmarks" is running, to resume the job if Chrome suspends the background process.

Where data is stored

All data (Favorites, Shortlist, click counts, exposure history, summary cache, settings and the API keys you enter) is stored only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). It is never synced to the developer or any server. Uninstalling the extension removes this data.

Optional feature: AI one-line summaries

  • This feature is off by default. It runs only after you read the privacy notice in Settings, tick the consent box, enter your own API key, and turn it on.
  • When on, the extension sends the title, domain and folder name of bookmarks that need a summary directly from your browser to the AI provider you choose to generate a one-line summary. Requests use your own API key and are billed to your account with that provider.
  • Built-in providers: DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com) and Google Gemini (generativelanguage.googleapis.com). Access to these two domains is granted at install.
  • You can also choose "Custom provider" and enter an endpoint. In that case the extension asks Chrome for access to that one domain only, and only when you click "Save", "Test connection" or "Fetch"; it never requests access to any other site. If you switch to a different endpoint, access to the old domain is revoked.
  • Never sent: full browsing history, page contents, cookies, passwords or any personally identifiable information.
  • How your chosen provider handles this data is governed by that provider's own privacy policy; please review it before use.
  • Summaries are cached locally by URL and are not sent again. The new tab page itself never calls AI; requests are sent only when you click "Start generating" or "Test connection". You can turn the feature off or withdraw consent at any time; to delete generated summaries, click "Clear summary cache" in Settings.

Support link

The coffee icon at the top right of Settings is only a support link: in Simplified Chinese it shows a WeChat donation QR image bundled with the extension; in other languages it opens Ko-fi (ko-fi.com). The extension includes no payment SDK and sends none of your data to these pages.

What we don't do

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information, browsing history or usage analytics.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • We do not sell or transfer user data, or use it for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Chrome Web Store User Data Policy

The use of user data by this extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children's privacy

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes to how data is used will be announced in the extension's update notes.
